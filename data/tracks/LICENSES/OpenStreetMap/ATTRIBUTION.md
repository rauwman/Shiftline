Those layout files are a database derived from OpenStreetMap data and are kept in their own folder. They are
NOT under the GPL licence of the Shiftline code: they are licensed under the ODbL 1.0 (full text: ODbL-1.0.txt in
this folder). Anyone may copy, use and adapt them under that licence; an adapted version that is made public has
to be offered under the ODbL as well. The OpenStreetMap way ids they follow are listed in provenance.correction.basis.wayIds.

Where a map made from these layouts is shown, "(c) OpenStreetMap contributors" has to be visible.

Changes made by Shiftline: shapes traced by eye along the OpenStreetMap ways, projected to metres,
smoothed with a centripetal Catmull-Rom spline and resampled to evenly spaced points.

Start/finish positions of these layouts are taken from OpenTrackData (https://github.com/chendo/opentrackdata),
researched there from official circuit sources: autopolis, bahrain, baku, mexico
